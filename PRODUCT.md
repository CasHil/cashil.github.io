- Preserves all authentic historical roles, education entries, and contact information without fabrication.

## Product Principles

1. **Craft over Clutter**: Every element serves clarity; avoid decorative gimmicks that distract from content.
2. **Authentic Voice**: Reflect real accomplishments and engineering perspective directly.
3. **Fluid Responsiveness**: Flawless reading and navigation on phones, tablets, and large displays alike.
4. **Performance & Accessibility**: Sub-second load times, semantic HTML, and accessible color contrast.
